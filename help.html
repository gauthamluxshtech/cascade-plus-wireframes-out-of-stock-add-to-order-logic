Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compare prices across wholesalers?

Navigate to the Order Pad, click on any product line in the Order Basket, and view the product details with the supplier comparison table. You can choose any supplier, and click Save. The basket will refresh with the updated quantity, pricing, and order value.

How do I add products to my order basket?

Go to the Order Pad, search for the product name or PIP code, enter the required quantity, and add it to the basket. You can also upload products using the Upload Order option if you have an .xls or .csv file or download the template.

How do I change the quantity of a product in my basket?

Click on any product line you want to change the quantity in the Order Basket, in the Edit product window change the quantity and click Save. The basket will refresh with the updated quantity, pricing, and order value.

What does "Out of Stock" mean?

Out of Stock means the selected supplier could not fulfil the requested product quantity. You can review these lines in the Out of Stock section and add selected items back to your order when needed.

Where can I view my previous orders?

Go to Order History to view past orders, order status, submitted products, fulfilled quantities, and supplier outcomes. Click an order to open the detailed view.
