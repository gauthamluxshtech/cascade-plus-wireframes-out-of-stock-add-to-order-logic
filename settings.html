General Limit

Applies to every product that does not have a product-specific limit.

Unsaved changes

The general limit is off. Only products with a product-specific limit show a warning in the Order Pad.

When an item's quantity for today (orders placed plus the basket) goes above its limit, its Order Pad line shows a warning sign. Hover over the sign for the details.

The warning never blocks an order. Adding, editing and placing orders work as before.

Product Specific Limit

Set a different quantity limit for selected products. It replaces the general limit for those products, and still applies when the general limit is off.
