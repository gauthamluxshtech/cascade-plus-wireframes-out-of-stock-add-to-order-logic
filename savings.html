How savings are calculated

Savings are calculated from the difference between the highest available supplier price and the price paid for the same product at the time of ordering.

Actual Saving = Highest Available Price - Price Paid
  • Orders with no price difference are marked as No Saving.
  • All figures are based on the selected filters.
